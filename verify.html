What a valid signature tells you

Issuing and checking completion codes need a separately approved release. Both are off on the public site now, so there may be nothing to check here yet.

When the service is enabled, a valid signature establishes one thing: this service issued these exact values and they were not edited afterwards. The score and active time were reported by the student's browser; the service did not watch the work. A valid signature does not establish identity, independent work, elapsed time, or a verified grade.

Authorship, attendance, the underlying score and mastery each need separate evidence, such as the lab report, a demonstration or a conversation. Review the student's lab report alongside the code.

Active time covers only the page visit in which the code was requested. A code requested after reopening finished work reports 0m 00s. That is expected.

Duplicate flags apply within this pasted batch. They are a prompt to review, not proof of misconduct.
