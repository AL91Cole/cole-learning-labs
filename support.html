Support the
free labs.

Cole Learning Labs has no ads, and right now every lab is open without an account. Mx. Cole builds and runs it. If it has helped you, your child or your class, you can choose to support the hosting costs and new labs with a small contribution through Buy Me a Coffee.

A contribution is optional and unlocks nothing: what you and your students can use stays the same.

Buy Me a Coffee

Scan the code with a phone camera, or open the page directly:

Open Buy Me a Coffee ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Both go to the same page: buymeacoffee.com/colelearninglabs

How it works

  • It is a separate service. Buy Me a Coffee handles any contribution, and its own terms and privacy policy apply to what you enter there, including payment details. This site sends it nothing. It only learns about your visit if you follow the link or scan the code.
  • Messages can be public. Buy Me a Coffee can show a supporter’s name and message on its page. Leave out student names and school details.
  • It is for adults. Students: you do not need to do anything here, and the labs are yours to use. If you liked a lab, tell your teacher. In student mode, the classroom link teachers share, this page and its links are hidden.
  • A contribution is never required. No lab, report or teaching resource asks for one, and contributing gives no extra access.

Other ways to help

  • Share a lab you liked with a teacher or a family. Teach a lesson has ready-to-run classroom lessons.
  • Suggest an improvement or report a problem on GitHub ↗. It is a public conversation, so use fictional examples only.
  • Tell Mx. Cole about an accessibility barrier at support@colelearninglabs.com.
