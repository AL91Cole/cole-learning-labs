Crack the note

  • The note. AOL OBUA ZAHYAZ HA AOL NFT KVVY
  • What the club says. Every letter moved forward the same number of places. That number is the key. Nobody wrote the key down.
  1. One three-letter word appears twice. Guess which common word it is. How far back must each letter move to make that word?
  2. Use your key on the whole note and write the message. Tally how many keys you tried. How many keys could there be at most?
  3. Pick your own key from 1 to 25. Scramble a three-word invented reply for a partner. Decide how to tell them the key so nobody else learns it, then trade.

Check the fingerprint

The adviser posted one rule, START AT NOON, with its fingerprint, 157. The club fingerprint adds the position of every letter. Two copies are going around.

  • Hallway copy. START AT NINE
  • Club board copy. START AT NOON
  1. Fingerprint both copies. Split the letters with a partner and add your totals. Which copy matches the adviser’s number?
  2. Everyone could read both copies the whole time. What did the fingerprint tell you? What can it not tell you about the person who made the change?

Take your key, your tally of tries and both fingerprints back to the lesson 4 handout.
