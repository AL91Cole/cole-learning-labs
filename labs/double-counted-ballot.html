The Double-Counted Ballot on paper

Use a separate sheet for your decisions. To print these records and prompts, use Print in your browser.

Read the election records

  • Club rule, signed before voting. Each anonymous ballot has its own receipt. Keep the first delivery for each receipt and set any later copies aside. The adviser confirms the next step in a tie.
  • Recorder’s display. Comet has 13 and Orbit has 12. Below are the original deliveries in arrival order.
  • AI draft. “Comet wins, 13 to 12!” The writing tool saw only the preliminary totals.
  • Hallway message. “Someone on the Comet team must have voted twice.” The message gives no witness or record.

Give the club your decision

  1. Name the two sources that establish the counting rule and the actual count. List the delivery numbers that led to your decision.
  2. Write a one-sentence public correction. Decide whether a winner can be announced and what can be said about the cause of the discrepancy.
  3. Write a private note for the adviser. What should they check next? Trade your work with a partner and recount any receipt on which you disagree.
