Teach a lesson

Your students are the tech team. They follow the clues, try a fix, and show how they know it worked. Pick a lesson below. Each one has a direct link, a realistic time, look-fors for student reasoning and a plan for a day without devices.

Everything is free to use. The people, messages, computers and networks in the labs are fictional.

Before you teach

No accounts

Every lesson on this page opens from its link. There is no sign-in, roster or class code to set up, and no student account to create.

Where student work stays

Work stays in the page while the page is open. In an investigation it is kept on the device only if the learner chooses "Save on this device". Inbox Defender and Packet Post keep nothing: progress resets when the student leaves the page.

Two classroom link modes

You can post either of these addresses, or both together, instead of the plain one.

ModeLink to postWhat it does
Student modehttps://www.colelearninglabs.com/?mode=studentHides the adult pages and turns off links to other websites.
Shared devicehttps://www.colelearninglabs.com/?cart=trueNever saves on the device. Clears the page after 40 minutes without activity, with a warning at 38 minutes.
Both togetherhttps://www.colelearninglabs.com/?mode=student&cart=trueStudent mode on a shared cart or lab device.

To open one lesson in a mode, put the mode before the lesson's own address, like this: https://www.colelearninglabs.com/?mode=student#lab/INC-101. Each lesson page prints its student-mode link for you.

These links are conveniences, not access control. A learner can still open the plain address.

How student work is collected

In The Blank Monitor, the student opens "My report" and reviews it for private details. The student ticks the review box, then copies it or downloads it as a .txt file and hands it in through your district's approved system. The report is not sent to this site, and nothing is submitted automatically.

Inbox Defender and Packet Post have no report. The evidence is what students say, and what they write or tell you for the exit question.

For your IT team

If your technology team needs to look at the site first, send them the district review page. It describes how the public site works. It is not an approval, a certification or a legal determination.

It also helps to run your lesson once on a student device, on the student network, before the class does.

How to share a lesson

Each lesson page ends with a share box. It holds the lesson link, a student-mode link and a short description you can paste into your learning management system or a class message.

Sharing is a link and text only. There is no roster, account or grade connection. The site cannot be shown inside a frame on another page, so post the link and let it open in its own tab.

Standards

No standards codes are listed on this page. Each lesson page names the New York State Computer Science and Digital Fluency learning standard it gives practice toward. It quotes the official statement and pairs it with one thing you can observe. Packet Post names a concept only, and its page says why.
