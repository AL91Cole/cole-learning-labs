Unmarked practice quizzes

Choose the form your teacher assigns. All eight original forms and their answer choices are included in their reviewed order. No answer key, scoring, or completed answers are included.

Use a fictional learner alias and class label. This page has no submission or saving code and sends no answers to Cole Learning Labs. Choices remain in this open page and can be lost when you leave or reload. Browser features or extensions may handle fields under their own settings. Follow your teacher’s approved route to share work; printing does not submit it.

Keyboard: use Tab to reach each question group, then the arrow keys to choose an answer. Space selects the focused choice. Use Tab and Shift+Tab to leave a group. To print, use your browser’s Print command (Ctrl+P on Windows/ChromeOS, Command+P on Mac) and choose the assigned form’s pages in print preview.
