Your privacy

The planned membership stores adult account and subscription details to manage access. Student work, chat history, names, grades and learning activity do not belong in billing records. Ordinary connection information is handled by hosting and payment services.

Google would handle sign-in and Stripe would handle checkout and billing. Final processing and retention notices need review before launch.

Renewal and cancellation

No price has been approved and no automatic charge can start from this preview. A future checkout must show the exact amount, renewal interval and cancellation method, with a separate, unchecked agreement.

The planned cancellation option is online through hosted billing. Canceling future renewals should preserve access through an eligible paid period.
