# Beginner labs

The catalog offers five beginner labs before the 29 investigation tickets. They are independent learning activities, not classroom assignment IDs or additional troubleshooting scenarios.

| Lab | Topic | Mini-job | Next ticket |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HW-100 · Meet the computer | Nine computer parts, their appearance and purpose | Fit memory, close clips and case screws, connect HDMI and AC, test startup and open the original lesson | INC-101 |
| NET-200 · Follow a network connection | Workstation, Ethernet, switch, router, DHCP, DNS and OS | Connect a fictional workstation, request automatic settings and test a practice page | NET-201 |
| AI-300 · Understand an AI response | Prompt, model, output, evidence, bias and human review | Choose fictional input, correct a claim against supplied evidence and complete human review | AI-301 |
| SEC-400 · Practice safe security habits | Scope, account identity, MFA, least privilege, phishing and reporting | Confirm fictional scope, enable simulated MFA and limit a learner role | SEC-401 |
| CODE-500 · Read, change, and test code | Inputs, variables, operators, outputs, fixtures and debugging | Change addition to multiplication and run quantities 0, 2 and 5 with one program | CODE-501 |

Routes use `#learn/hardware`, `#learn/network`, `#learn/ai`, `#learn/security`, and `#learn/coding`. The matching uppercase lab codes resolve to the same page-memory record. Catalog and home links introduce the activities without a 3D room.

## Learning flow

**Explore** shows pictures, short appearance cues and the job each item does. Selecting a picture, a diagram component or its ordinary item button records inspection. An original SVG portrait makes hardware recognizable even when a fitted cooler covers the CPU. Looking or choosing a tool does not repair equipment or complete practice.

**Practice** presents three role questions one at a time, with feedback and an explicit Next control. After every item is explored and those checks are correct, a short mini-job shows one current action. Hardware uses the actual existing PC assembly model, with its power, clip, mount, screw and original-file checks. The prepared PC is off and unplugged, with its case open and memory in the tray. Every guided action has a large ordinary button. A still 2D schematic reflects the actual model state; no camera, engine loading or WebGL is required.

The other topics use native illustrated buttons and bounded fictional state changes. Coding uses the existing restricted interpreter rather than browser execution; all three outputs are recomputed from the current source. An edit invalidates earlier results. No external AI provider, real scan, operating-system command, upload or credentials are used.

**Check** summarizes exploration, role checks and the mini-job, then offers a practice report and the next ticket. An unfinished report can also be exported. Completion is public learning feedback, not a tamper-proof grade or a private teacher answer key.

## Work and reports

Beginner records live only in a separate page-memory map. Normal hash navigation keeps progress; they never enter investigation `localStorage` or the classroom sharing store. Closing or leaving the document clears both the records and cached learning markup even when optional investigation device saving is enabled. Browsers may show an unsaved-work warning after meaningful beginner work. Export first; the site cannot recover a closed session.

Reports reuse the existing reviewed export dialog: approval before plaintext download/copy, optional local teacher-key encryption, and manual submission through a school-approved destination. Filenames contain only the authored lab code, such as `cole-learning-labs-HW-100.txt`. No student identity is requested. Hosting still receives ordinary web requests as described in the site's privacy information; page-memory work is not a promise of zero infrastructure data.

The real classroom-record production privacy gate and unavailable live-AI mode are unchanged. Beginner practice does not enable accounts, provider calls, roster access or submissions.
