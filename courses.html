Courses for your classroom

Available courses

  • Intro to Cybersecurity and Responsible AI

    Grades 7–8 · About three weeks · 16 lessons · 40 minutes per lesson

    A scrambled note falls out of a locker. A message wants your sign-in code in two minutes. An AI-generated answer sounds right. What would you check before you trust any of them? Four units of fictional school cases cover protecting accounts and files, how messages travel and stay secret, spotting tricks and reporting incidents, how AI learns, and where AI goes wrong.

    Every lesson opens with a short warm-up challenge and ends with a named skill. In four hands-on investigations, students crack a cipher, audit an election tally, train a sorting robot and edit an AI-written museum label. In the final project, students edit a club bulletin for safe sharing. When new information arrives, they correct the bulletin and show readers which source they used.

  • Technology, AI & Cybersecurity Foundations

    Grades 7–8 · Nine weeks · 45 lessons · 40 minutes per lesson

    Build from computer basics and systematic troubleshooting to networks, cybersecurity, privacy, responsible AI and introductory coding. Finish with a project that brings the skills together.

    Each lesson includes a timed teaching sequence, a lab or hands-on activity, student questions, an exit ticket, assessment guidance, support and challenge options, and a supplied no-device alternative.

    Share public lesson links through Google Classroom. Students submit through the school’s Classroom assignment, and teachers review and grade there.
