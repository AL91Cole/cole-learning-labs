    Technology, AI & Cybersecurity Foundations

    Grades 7–8 · Nine weeks · 45 lessons · 40 minutes per lesson

    Build from computer basics and systematic troubleshooting to networks, cybersecurity, privacy, responsible AI and introductory coding. Finish with a project that brings the skills together.

    Each lesson includes a timed teaching sequence, a lab or hands-on activity, student questions, an exit ticket, assessment guidance, support and challenge options, and a supplied no-device alternative.

    Share public lesson links through Google Classroom. Students submit through the school’s Classroom assignment, and teachers review and grade there.

    Open the course Technology, AI & Cybersecurity Foundations→Preview lesson 1

    www.colelearninglabs.com/courses/foundations

This first edition is ready for teacher review and local classroom piloting. It has not been field-tested with a class or approved by a district. Preview the activities on your school’s devices and network before teaching.

More courses are planned

More courses will be added here as they are developed. This catalog currently contains one course; no release dates are set for additional courses.

Your school keeps the classroom records

The course uses public links without Cole Learning Labs student accounts, roster imports, or student submission and grade storage. The teacher posts the lesson, collects student work and records grades in the school’s Google Classroom environment. Sharing a link does not submit work or sync grades.

Lab work may be held temporarily in the browser. Follow each lesson’s saving and submission instructions, and use fictional details in simulations. For hosting, browser storage and optional-service details, see the district review and School IT review.

Teaching one lesson?

The Teach a lesson page still has three featured lessons with direct activity links, timing and unplugged fallbacks. Use the teaching toolkit to adapt a lesson or plan a particular investigation.
