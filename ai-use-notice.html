Before asking a question

Do not enter personal or confidential information. Use fictional examples. Leave out real names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, school or student IDs, passwords, access codes, health information and private incidents. Do not share another person's information. Ask your teacher for help if you are unsure.

A reminder cannot prevent every disclosure. Automated checks can miss private details. School-managed devices, browser extensions and networks can have their own visibility. Do not treat a chat box as a confidential place.

Educational practice and AI limits

The labs and requester characters are fictional educational simulations. Extra replies can be playful and brief, followed by a reminder to return to the task. They may be incorrect, incomplete or unsuitable for a real situation. Authored case evidence and your teacher's instructions take priority. Extra chat does not earn evidence, verify a repair, award progress or determine a grade.

Do not rely on these replies for professional advice, real security incidents or decisions about someone's health, safety, rights or records. Use the appropriate teacher, school process or qualified professional.

What the current chat sends

Typed questions stay on this device in the current release. Optional cloud replies start off on a fresh page. Choosing them can send only a fictional lab ID and predefined topic code through the website to its private Cloudflare Worker. Raw questions, notes, reports and conversation history are not sent to the model. Cloudflare still processes ordinary connection and service information. See the full privacy information.

General open-ended cloud questions are not enabled. They require a separate legal, insurance, school, provider and safety review before typed questions may leave the device. Improvement reporting and connected classroom collection remain disabled.
